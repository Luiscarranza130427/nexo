# Product

Nexo is an internal operations platform for NovaTec and other small teams.

Small teams run their operations across scattered spreadsheets, chat threads and
shared folders. Nexo replaces that with a single place where the work lives and
stays connected.

## Who it is for

Small teams — starting with NovaTec itself — that need a shared operational view
without the cost and complexity of enterprise software.

## What it will centralize

- **Clients** — the companies and contacts the team works with.
- **Projects** — the work delivered for those clients.
- **Tasks** — the day-to-day units of work inside a project.
- **Team** — people, roles and availability.
- **Documents** — files and references attached to clients and projects.
- **Finances** — budgets, invoices and payment tracking.
- **Calendar** — deadlines, meetings and milestones.
- **Notifications** — what changed and what needs attention.
- **Activity** — an auditable history of what happened and who did it.

## Current state

None of the above is implemented yet. The repository currently holds the monorepo
skeleton, a minimal frontend and a minimal API. See [ROADMAP.md](./ROADMAP.md)
for the order in which these areas will be built.
